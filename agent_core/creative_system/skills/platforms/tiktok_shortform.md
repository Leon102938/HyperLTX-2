## title
TikTok Shortform Skill

## purpose
Shape fast portrait videos with immediate visual hooks and clear repeated-action value.

## when_to_use
Use for short social videos aimed at TikTok-style feed viewing.

## rules
- Open with a visible change in the first beat.
- Keep scenes readable on a phone screen.
- Favor one action per shot.

## do
- Use portrait framing and strong subject clarity.
- Build a compact hook, development beat, and payoff.
- Make motion legible without relying on text overlays.

## dont
- Do not create static lifestyle filler.
- Do not rely on small readable labels.
- Do not use multi-panel layouts unless explicitly requested.

## output_contract
- Platform plan identifies hook, retention beat, and payoff.
- Review checks include platform_fit and weak_hook.

## common_failures
- Pretty but slow opening.
- Generic stock b-roll with no action.
- Visuals require subtitles to make sense.

## audit_hints
- Inspect scene_plan.json first scene for opening_emphasis.
- Check final_quality_verdict for platform-fit weakness.
