## title
Instagram Reels Skill

## purpose
Shape polished portrait clips with clean composition, aspirational clarity, and repeatable actions.

## when_to_use
Use for lifestyle, product, or tip videos intended for Instagram Reels.

## rules
- Prioritize polished framing and visually satisfying transitions.
- Keep content understandable without generated text.
- Maintain a coherent style lock.

## do
- Use clean light, tactile details, and simple movement.
- Keep props minimal and intentional.
- Preserve color and texture consistency.

## dont
- Do not overpack the frame.
- Do not use fake UI or text surfaces.
- Do not create editorial collage unless requested.

## output_contract
- Platform plan records visual polish targets and composition checks.
- Review includes composition and generic_stock_feel checks.

## common_failures
- Looks like stock b-roll.
- Motifs do not connect across beats.
- Composition is too busy for mobile.

## audit_hints
- Compare style_lock in director_output.json with model_prompts.json.
