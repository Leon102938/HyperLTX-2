## title
YouTube Shorts Skill

## purpose
Shape short vertical videos with a clear premise, visual progression, and watch-to-end payoff.

## when_to_use
Use for Shorts-style educational, product, or lifestyle clips.

## rules
- Make the topic visually clear early.
- Give each beat a reason to continue.
- Avoid visual ambiguity when narration is present.

## do
- Use clear beat progression.
- Keep actions large enough for mobile viewing.
- Use final beat as a payoff or resolved state.

## dont
- Do not hide the subject in atmosphere.
- Do not depend on tiny text.
- Do not loop unrelated b-roll.

## output_contract
- Beat plan includes premise, progression, and payoff.
- Review includes unclear_action and weak_payoff checks.

## common_failures
- Voice explains one thing while visuals show another.
- Middle beat has no progression.
- Payoff is visually weak.

## audit_hints
- Inspect beat plan metadata and final_quality_verdict.
