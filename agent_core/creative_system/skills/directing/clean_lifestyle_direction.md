## title
Clean Lifestyle Direction Skill

## purpose
Keep lifestyle visuals calm, tactile, uncluttered, and non-generic.

## when_to_use
Use for Morning Reset, focus, wellness, and routine-oriented modes.

## rules
- Use ordinary physical actions with premium restraint.
- Keep props few and purposeful.
- Make calm scenes still contain visible change.

## do
- Use natural light, fabric, water, surfaces, breath, and small gestures.
- Protect against phones, screens, labels, paper, and fake text.
- Keep environment believable.

## dont
- Do not force a curtain/water/window sequence for every idea.
- Do not make beige stock imagery with no action.
- Do not add decorative UI or labels.

## output_contract
- Visual direction includes tactile motif, action, lighting, and hard bans.

## common_failures
- Clean but boring.
- Phone/UI appears near objects.
- Motif feels copied between runs.

## audit_hints
- Inspect motif_family and shot_recipe fields in prompt_audit.json.
