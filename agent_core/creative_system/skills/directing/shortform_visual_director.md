## title
Shortform Visual Director Skill

## purpose
Direct short videos as mobile-first visual sequences with strong opening action.

## when_to_use
Use for social shortform pipelines and director planning.

## rules
- Make the first beat visually active.
- Keep every beat legible without captions.
- Avoid stock b-roll filler.

## do
- Choose clear subject-action-environment combinations.
- Vary shot scale across beats.
- Keep payoff visually obvious.

## dont
- Do not make all scenes calm static interiors.
- Do not rely on generated text.
- Do not overcomplicate with too many props.

## output_contract
- Director output records hook, visual progression, payoff, and risks.

## common_failures
- Clip is boring.
- Weak hook.
- Visuals do not match the voice.

## audit_hints
- Check scene_intents in director_output.json.
