## title
Anti Boring Scene Skill

## purpose
Prevent technically clean but low-value scenes from passing planning and review.

## when_to_use
Use in creative strategy, beat planning, visual direction, and quality review.

## rules
- A scene must have action, contrast, reveal, tension, or payoff.
- Static ambience alone is not enough.
- Generic stock feel is a reviewable weakness.

## do
- Add a micro-change: light changes, hand enters, object placed, fabric moves, body resets.
- Use closer details when wide scenes feel bland.
- Check opening beat for immediate interest.

## dont
- Do not accept empty rooms as a hook.
- Do not repeat identical calm shots.
- Do not overfix boring scenes with text overlays.

## output_contract
- Plans and reviews carry boring_scene and weak_hook checks.

## common_failures
- Clip is boring.
- Beautiful but pointless b-roll.
- Final verdict approves weak visuals.

## audit_hints
- Inspect final_quality_verdict, takes.json review issues, and scene_plan.json actions.
